# Worked example: a timed PM take-home

The first session logged with this format. The exercise was to diagnose checkout abandonment at the payment step plus rising order cancellations on a B2B marketplace, and to write a short product brief and an honest note on how AI was used. Budget was 1–2 hours. Andrew worked with Claude Code.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`session_log.md`](session_log.md) | The full record: 43-row timeline, 15 verbatim prompts, 7 course-corrections, attribution, time |
| [`retrospective.md`](retrospective.md) | The one-page "how I used AI" note from the final deliverable |

**Read the retrospective first,** then the log's course-corrections if you want the detail behind it.

## The short version

- 137 minutes elapsed, about 77 active: ~12 setup, ~20 substance, ~45 format.
- 7 course-corrections, 6 of them on form.
- The AI kept optimizing for completeness and fitting the page. The human kept optimizing for the reader.
- The fix the log pointed to was a template, which is what this repo is.

## What was sanitized

The company, the exercise text and platform-specific claims are removed or generalized. Redactions in the verbatim prompts are marked with [square brackets]. The exercise PDF, the brief and the working notes aren't included. Tag and layer names have been updated to the repo's scheme. Nothing else was changed.
